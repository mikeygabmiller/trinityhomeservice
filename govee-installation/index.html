Govee installation questions

How much does Govee light installation cost in Bothell or Mill Creek?
The quote depends on the roofline length, height, access, model and connections needed. Send your city and phone number, then share exterior photos by text. Louis will confirm the scope and price before you book. No fixed per-foot price is promised on this page.
Should I choose Govee Elite or Pro?
Elite may suit continuous eaves where complete factory sections fit. Pro supports manufacturer-approved cutting and splicing, which can help with separated rooflines and exact endpoints. The exact model number, power layout and manual matter more than the product name alone.
Can you install lights I already own?
Tell Louis the exact model number and kit length if you already own lights. Product compatibility and whether your kit can be used need to be confirmed before booking. If you have not purchased lights, discuss the layout first; the quote will specify who supplies the product.
Are permanent outdoor lights the same as Christmas lights?
Permanent outdoor lights are designed to remain installed and provide different colors or white-light scenes throughout the year. Seasonal Christmas lighting is a separate service. Its takedown, storage and seasonal repair terms do not automatically apply to a permanent Govee installation.
Will the lights work with my roofline and outdoor outlet?
Send photos of the eaves, upper peaks, gaps between roof sections and the available outdoor outlet. Model limits, mounting surfaces and access need review. Any required electrical work or HOA approval should be resolved before installation.
What warranty or maintenance is included?
Ask Louis to specify installation coverage, maintenance and any product warranty terms in the written quote. Manufacturer coverage depends on the exact product and its terms. This page does not promise a lifetime warranty or free permanent-light repairs.
