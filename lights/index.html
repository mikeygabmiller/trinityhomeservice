Before You Book

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does holiday light installation cost?
It depends on the size of your home, how much of it you want lit and how easy the roofline is to reach. Send us your details and Louis will prepare a quote for your home.
Do you supply the lights, or do I?
We supply commercial-grade LED lights custom-cut to your home, and that's included in your quote. If you'd rather we hang lights you already own, just tell us — we'll take a look and let you know what we can safely work with.
When do you install, and when do you take them down?
Call or send the quote form for current installation dates. Takedown and off-season storage are included.
Will the clips damage my roof or gutters?
No. We use plastic clips that grip your shingles and gutter lips. No nails, no staples, no drilling, and they come off clean at takedown.
What happens if a section stops working?
Call or text us and we'll come out and fix it at no extra charge. Repairs during the season are covered by our Stays-Lit Guarantee.
What areas do you serve?
Snohomish County and the towns around it: Snohomish, Monroe, Lake Stevens, Everett, Marysville, Mill Creek, Mukilteo and more.
Do you also do exterior cleaning?
We do. Windows, gutters, roof soft-washing and pressure washing, all year round. Plenty of homeowners have us clean the gutters on the same visit as the light install. See our cleaning services →
