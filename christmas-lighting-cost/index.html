What should a lighting quote cover?

A photo and a clear description of the rooflines you want help define the work. Ask for the final design, scope and price together so you know exactly what you are choosing.

The roof edges

Front-facing eaves, garage edges and peaks are different parts of the design. Mark the sections you want included instead of using your home’s floor area as the lighting measurement.

The access

Tell Louis the number of stories and show the roof shape from the ground. Leave measuring high edges and checking access to the professional.

The season’s service

Trinity supplies custom-cut, commercial-grade LEDs mounted on clips. Mid-season repairs, takedown and off-season storage are included.

Christmas lighting quote questions

Can I get an exact price from a house photo?

A photo helps explain your preferred design. Louis will confirm the rooflines, access and final price for your property. This planner prepares the conversation; it is not a binding estimate.

Do I need to buy the lights?

Trinity supplies the commercial-grade LEDs and custom-cuts them for the installation. Takedown and off-season storage are included.

Can I start with just the front of the house?

You can use the planner to request front-facing coverage. Louis will confirm the design and which sections the quote includes.

What if a light needs attention during the season?

Mid-season repairs are included. Contact Louis to describe the issue.
